How the gap is computed
  • Starter home: Zillow Home Value Index, bottom third of homes, each city, Sep 30, 2026.
  • What the family can pay: Orange County area median income for 4 people, $138,600 (HCD 2026 State Income Limits, revised Jun 23, 2026). Moderate: 35% × 110% of it per year on housing. Lower: 30% × 70%. Per HSC 50052.5 as summarized by HCD; check the statute text before relying on it.
  • Turned into a price at the chosen rate, 30-year fixed. ASSUMPTION: 3% down, property tax 1.1%/yr, insurance 0.35%/yr, no HOA.
  • Dream For All: 20% of price, capped at $150k, per CalHFA's program page. Whether funds are open was not confirmed.
  • Gap clock: 12-month change in the same Zillow index, divided by 12.
  • Seal Beach and Laguna Woods are excluded: their low-end homes are mostly in age-restricted communities.
