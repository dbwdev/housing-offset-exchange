    The Housing Offset Standard · draft 0.1

    Why buyers can trust a unit

    Carbon markets earned trust with units, registries and audits. We borrow the playbook. One rule differs.

    Unit1 tonne CO₂e1 Home Offset Unit: one working household under 120% of area median income, in an ownership home near its job
    AdditionalityWould the cut have happened anyway?Income certified against the dated HCD table; public programs stacked first; only the remaining gap is sold
    PermanenceThe forest stays standingA shared-equity stake recorded on title; the worker's share vests the longer they stay

    From concept to market

    Six steps to make it real

    1. Pilot three homes

      Months 0–6

      One OC employer, one land trust, three workers. Measure retention.

    2. Publish the standard

      Months 0–6

      Additionality, permanence and verification as a public, versioned document.

    3. Legal and tax wrapper

      Months 3–9

      Shared-appreciation note; opinions on loan vs wages and deduction treatment.

    4. Launch the registry

      Months 6–12

      Serial-numbered homes on a public ledger. No personal data.

    5. Disclosure recognition

      Year 1–2

      CRA examiners, CARB commuting rules, Schedule H reviewers, social bond verifiers.

    6. Pool many employers

      Year 2+

      A multi-employer fund so a 400-person company can buy one unit.
